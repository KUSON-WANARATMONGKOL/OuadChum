Our Philosophy

OuadChum Philosophy: Business to Help

Business to Help means building a business that helps communities make the most of the resources and support they already have.

We work with universities and state agencies to turn their research and community networks into real, on-the-ground practice - starting right here in Chumphon.

5
Core Dimensions of Development

Our core framework is Holistic Area Based Community Development (HAB), striving to build a sustainable model at the sub-district level.
