01

Behind Every Dish

At our kitchen, we use only locally sourced ingredients — kapi (fermented krill paste) from Aunty Phin and her local network, and seafood from the fishermen at Bang Son pier for freshness every day. Vegetables come from our own garden and neighboring farmers whenever we can, and we use quality pork, chicken, and eggs from Betagro.

02

From Ground Up

Our pesticide-free vegetables started small — just enough to feed ourselves — before growing into every spare patch of land on the property, tended by hand by our housekeepers. We compost our own food scraps and fallen leaves for fertilizer, and buy the rest from the community and Ban Hin Kop School. They may not look picture-perfect, but they're completely chemical-free — sweet, crisp, and full of flavor.

03

From The Sea

Baan OuadChum's seafood comes straight from local small-scale fishermen — small boats, caught day by day — so every ingredient stays fresh and full of flavor, true to the local way of life. We buy by the season and never stockpile until the taste fades, cooking everything simply so the main ingredient can speak for itself.

Local Ingredients

Behind Every Cup

01
Pi Jongkoy

Wildflower Honey

Wild honey from the South, harvested from a mixed-fruit orchard. This tunnel-hive honey carries a complex flavor — sweet with a hint of sourness. Uncle Jongkoy harvests it the conservation way, without harming the young bees, and we use his honey to add gentle sweetness across our entire Non-Coffee menu.

02
Jak Jai Group

Nipa Palm Syrup

A rare, old-style healthy sugar made from the sweet sap of nipa palm flower clusters — fragrant, with a hint of salt and caramel. It’s the healthy sweetener behind our Es-Yen (Thai-style iced coffee).

03
A New Generation of Farmers

J&O Cacao

J&O crafts premium chocolate using only the Chumphon Hybrid 1 variety, rich in good fats extracted through cold-pressing with no added palm oil — better for your health. It carries a fragrant white-flower aroma, grown and processed 100% in Pathio, for an intensely rich flavor with just a hint of tartness.

04
Uncle Kanchit

Coconut Sugar

Sweet sap tapped from coconut palm blossoms, slow-simmered over a wood fire until it thickens into sugar. Uncle Kanchit makes it entirely by hand using traditional methods from start to finish — this sweetness is a key ingredient throughout Baan OuadChum.
